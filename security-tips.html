Cybersecurity Starts With Awareness

You do not need to be a cybersecurity expert to stay safe. Simple habits like checking links, using strong passwords, and verifying unexpected messages can protect you from many common online scams.

IMPORTANT SECURITY TIPS

Protect Your Digital Life

Follow these important security practices every day.

🔐

Use Strong Passwords

Use long and unique passwords for your important accounts. Avoid using the same password everywhere.

🔗

Check Links Before Opening

Be careful with unfamiliar links in messages, emails, social media, and QR codes.

📱

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Add an extra layer of protection to your important accounts whenever the option is available.

⚠️

Beware of Urgent Messages

Scammers often create fear or urgency to make you act without thinking. Stop and verify first.

🔄

Keep Devices Updated

Regularly update your phone, computer, browser, and applications to receive security improvements.

🏦

Verify Bank Messages

Do not trust unexpected banking messages immediately. Contact your bank using official channels.

💳

Think Before Paying

Verify unexpected payment requests before sending money or entering payment information.

🔒

Never Share OTPs

Never share your OTP, PIN, password, or other authentication information with unknown people.

🚨 Common Scam Warning Signs

Be careful when you notice these signs in a message, email, website, or payment request.

⏰

Extreme Urgency

"Act now!", "Account blocked today!", or "Payment required immediately!"

Warning
🎭

Impersonation

Someone claims to be a bank, company, delivery service, or government organization.

Verify
🔗

Suspicious Links

The website address looks unusual or different from the official organization website.

Check
🔑

Requests for Sensitive Information
